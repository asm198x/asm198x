# Retain deferred alias controls (#791)

The 62 native ca65/ld65 probes yield 47 accepted and 15 refused outcomes. The
explicit preceding record-fix executable disagrees on 47. Identity and every
process/status/byte result are retained in native-probes.json and
predecessor-red.json. All native calls completed within their ten-second bound.

The accepted controls include root/scoped forward constants and labels,
forward arithmetic, alias chains that later become source-time constants,
PC aliases before/after bytes and org, cheap locals, exported aliases and
zero-page aliases. Native refuses unused undefined aliases, unused cycles,
self cycles, imported definitions and duplicates. Dead definitions stay dead.
Formatter/debug/diagnostic coverage and implementation are still pending.

The separate numeric-token issue #788 has 64 additional retained probes in
deferred-numeric-boundaries.json, generated by probe_numeric_boundaries.py.
Twenty are accepted and 44 refused. Native accepts decimal 4294967296 and
4294967299, while rejecting the equivalent hex-prefix, hex-suffix and binary
literals; decimal 4294967300 is refused too. Both unused-alias and positive
empty-tag contexts preserve that distinction. Do not impose a generic 32-bit
integer limit or a tagged-field count ceiling to repair the lexical gap.
